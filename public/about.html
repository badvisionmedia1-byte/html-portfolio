About Me

I'm an IT professional, web developer, and video editor with a strong interest in technology, creative media, and problem solving. I enjoy learning how things work, troubleshooting technical issues, and turning ideas into finished projects.

Through BAD Vision Media, I focus on web development and post-production. My work includes building websites, editing video, creating digital content, and continuing to expand my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, networking, and other technologies.

As BAD Vision Media continues to grow, I want that growth to mean something beyond the business itself. My plan is to donate 10% of the proceeds to a children's charity, so that every project completed can also help support children who need a little more opportunity, care, and hope.
