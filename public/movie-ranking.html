The Best Movies According to BAD Vision Media

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Empire Strikes Back

My favorite Star Wars movie. Great story, characters, and one of the best endings in the series.

Dead Poet Society

One of my favorite Robin Williams films. It showed that he could go far beyond comedy and deliver a powerful, serious performance.

American Graffiti

I love the classic cars, the music, and the feeling of cruising around on a summer night.
